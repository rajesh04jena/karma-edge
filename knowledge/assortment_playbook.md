# Assortment & Allocation Playbook (internal)

## Section 1 — Climate fit rule
Cold-weather SKUs (snow boots, insulated outerwear, thermal base layers) may be
allocated to hot-climate stores only up to 3% of the national buy, and only for
travel-demand doors. Allocation above 3% into a hot climate is an
**assortment error** owned by Buying, regardless of who signed the allocation file.

## Section 2 — Weeks of cover targets
| Situation | Target cover |
| --- | --- |
| Core replenishment | 6–10 weeks |
| Seasonal in-season | 4–8 weeks |
| Seasonal end-of-season | < 3 weeks |
| Long lead time (> 40 days) | 8–12 weeks |

Below 2 weeks is a stockout risk owned by Supply Chain. Above 12 weeks is
trapped capital owned by Buying.

## Section 3 — Lead time discipline
Any vendor with lead time above 40 days requires a forecast with a documented
backtest error (MAPE) attached to the buy. A buy placed without a backtest is
un-defendable and the resulting markdown belongs to the buyer, not the market.

## Section 4 — Newness cadence
No more than 35% of category units may sit in SKUs older than 3 seasons. Aged
depth suppresses full-price sell-through on new receipts; the cost is charged to
Buying, not to Pricing.

## Section 5 — Attribution honesty
A margin finding must name one owning function. "Cross-functional" is not an
owner. If two functions contributed, split the dollars explicitly and log two
findings.
