# Markdown & Discount Policy (internal)

## Section 1 — Purpose
Discounting is a tool, not a personality trait. This policy defines the maximum
markdown depth by category and the approvals required to exceed it.

## Section 2 — Maximum depth without approval
| Category | Max discount | Approver above cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| apparel | 30% | Category Director |
| footwear | 25% | Category Director |
| outerwear | 40% (in-season: 15%) | VP Merchandising |
| smallappliance | 15% | VP Merchandising + Finance |
| electronics | 10% | VP Merchandising + Finance |

## Section 3 — Escalating markdown is a planning failure
Any SKU whose average discount rises for three consecutive months is deemed a
**buy-quantity error**, not a pricing decision. Ownership of the margin loss
transfers to the Buying function at the third consecutive month. Pricing may not
be charged with a leak created by an over-buy.

## Section 4 — Terminal markdown
Clearance below unit cost requires Finance sign-off and must be logged with the
original buy decision reference. "It was aged" is not a reference.

## Section 5 — Price parity
We do not chase competitor prices below a 6% gap. Gaps above 12% on top-100
revenue SKUs must be reviewed within 10 business days of detection.

## Section 6 — Evidence standard
A markdown recommendation must state: units at risk, weeks of cover, elasticity
estimate with observation count, and the margin dollars protected versus the
margin dollars conceded. Recommendations without all five are returned unread.
